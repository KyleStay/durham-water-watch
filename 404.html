Why these can differ

Drought describes regional dryness. Durham’s stage also reflects usable storage, demand, treatment and distribution conditions, forecasts, and management decisions. One does not mechanically set the other.

Read the official response plan, Tables 5.1–5.2 ↗

How the City’s forecast is described

  1. Model usable storage.The plan uses combined usable reservoir storage—not individual elevation percentages—as the key forecast input.
  2. Project demand and system conditions.The City considers demand, lake elevations, intake access, treatment and distribution capacity, neighboring supplies, weather, and other operational factors.
  3. Test the 10-week probability.The model estimates the chance of reaching the combined-storage target by the end of the forecast horizon.
  4. Apply management judgment.The City may change stages non-sequentially, act earlier, or rescind more slowly as conditions warrant.

What does not measure closeness

  • 195 estimated days of supply is not an exit threshold.
  • The NC drought category does not mechanically set the City stage.
  • Feet below full cannot be converted to percent storage without official storage curves.
  • Streamflow cannot predict reservoir refill or a stage change.

If Durham publishes the modeled probability and combined-storage forecast, this dashboard can show that official outlook directly and compare it with the plan—without inventing a forecast.

What to do now

Stage 2 rules, at a glance.

Plain-language summary of the currently verified City page. The complete official rules control.

×Do not

  • No landscape spray irrigation with City water, including hose-end sprinklers.
  • No vehicle washing, except at a commercial or institutional car wash.
  • No washing sidewalks, driveways, decks, exterior walls, or paved areas.
  • Do not add water to decorative fountains, ponds, or pools unless recycled.

✓Allowed alternatives

  • Hand watering, drip irrigation, and tree or shrub watering bags.
  • Watering container plants and commercial plant stock for sale.
  • Surface washing for a health or safety issue, or before painting.
  • Pool water only for evaporation or spillage loss and chemical feed operation.

iOther Stage 2 rules

  • Restaurants serve drinking water only when requested.
  • Users above 100,000 gallons/day should target a 30% reduction and document efforts.
  • Approved Water Conservation Plans and limited exemptions may apply.
  • Violations may lead to civil penalties or service termination.

This summary does not replace the official rules.

Sources & methodology

Trust is a product feature.

This dashboard links every operational metric to its authoritative source, keeps the last verified value when refreshes fail, and never substitutes an estimate.

01

Meaning, not mystery

Days of supply combines accessible reservoir water, less-accessible water below the intakes, and Teer Quarry emergency storage. Elevation is never presented as percent storage.

02

Refresh rhythm

A scheduled publication workflow checks Durham daily, USGS about every 30 minutes, and NC drought after its weekly update, then rebuilds the static site.

03

Stale thresholds

Durham daily metrics: two calendar days. USGS: about three hours. NC drought: after the expected weekly update window.

04

Validation before publication

Expected URL and units, a recognizable date, nonnegative components, internally consistent totals, intended reservoir fields, newer observations, and plausible changes.

05

Failure is visible

Malformed, older, null, or implausible readings never replace the repository’s last-known-good snapshot. The next static build keeps the verified value with a stale label; without one, it says reading unavailable.

06

Limits

City chart images are not digitized. Streamflow is not used to estimate storage or refill. Trend claims require at least two locally verified readings.
